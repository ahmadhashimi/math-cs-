# Code of Conduct

## Our pledge

This is a project about learning mathematics people were told they were bad at.
That makes the tone of this repository part of the work, not separate from it.

We pledge to make participation a harassment-free experience for everyone,
regardless of age, body size, visible or invisible disability, ethnicity, sex
characteristics, gender identity and expression, level of experience, education,
socio-economic status, nationality, personal appearance, race, religion, or
sexual identity and orientation.

## Our standards

Behaviour that builds this community:

- Assuming a question is asked in good faith, and answering it that way.
- Being specific rather than dismissive. "This is wrong because the discriminant
  is negative here" helps; "this is obviously wrong" does not.
- Accepting that not knowing something is the normal state of a learner, and
  never treating it as a failing. Nobody arrives knowing this.
- Giving and gracefully accepting constructive feedback.
- Taking responsibility for mistakes, apologising, and learning from them.

Behaviour that is not acceptable:

- Sexualised language or imagery, and sexual attention or advances of any kind.
- Trolling, insulting or derogatory comments, and personal or political attacks.
- Public or private harassment.
- Publishing others' private information without explicit permission.
- Condescension about someone's level — including the "how do you not know
  this?" register. It is the single fastest way to make someone stop learning,
  and it is not welcome here.

## Enforcement

Instances of abusive, harassing, or otherwise unacceptable behaviour may be
reported to the project maintainers at Fannos Academy. All complaints will be
reviewed and investigated promptly and fairly, and the privacy and security of
the reporter will be respected.

Maintainers may remove, edit, or reject comments, commits, code, issues and
other contributions that are not aligned with this Code of Conduct, and will
communicate reasons for moderation decisions when appropriate. Repeated or
serious violations may result in a temporary or permanent ban from any sort of
interaction with the project.
